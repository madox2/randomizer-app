Randomizer is the all-in-one random generator for every decision, game and argument. Flip a coin, roll dice, pick a random number, draw cards, spin the bottle or split your friends into fair random teams. Fast, free, no sign-up, works offline.

🎲 EVERYTHING RANDOM IN ONE APP

• RANDOM NUMBER GENERATOR – set your own range and get a random number instantly. Perfect for raffles, giveaways, lotteries and picking a winner.
• COIN FLIP – heads or tails? Toss a virtual coin to settle any dispute.
• DICE ROLLER – roll one or many dice with any number of sides, from a classic D6 to D20, D100 and beyond. Great for board games and tabletop RPGs like D&D.
• RANDOM TEAM GENERATOR / TEAM MAKER – add your players once, choose the number of teams and shuffle them into fair, random teams. Your player list is saved for next time. Ideal for football, basketball, volleyball, esports, classrooms and game night.
• PLAYING CARDS – draw from a shuffled 52-card deck without putting cards back, just like the real thing.
• SPIN THE BOTTLE – spin it and see who it points at. A classic party game for friends.
• MAGIC 8-BALL – ask a yes/no question and get your answer.
• DRAW MATCHES – pull a match and whoever gets the burned one loses. Perfect for choosing who goes first, who pays or who does the chores.

• Smooth animations and haptic feedback
• Light and dark theme that follows your phone
• Works offline – no internet connection needed
• No account, no sign-up

• Picking a random winner for a contest or giveaway

• Party games, drinking games and truth or dare

Whether you need a coin toss, a dice roll, a random picker or a random team shuffle, Randomizer has it in one lightweight app. Download it now and let chance decide!
